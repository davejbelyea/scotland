The idea

A little city.
A lot of scenery.

Start slowly in Edinburgh, then trade city lanes for mountain passes and the west coast. Spend a few nights on Skye, where the days are shaped by the weather, the light and whichever turn looks promising.

This route keeps the number of hotel changes low and gives you more than one night in each place. The one long drive comes at the end, with a final Edinburgh stay before flying home.

Working dates: fly Boston to Edinburgh overnight on May 4, arrive May 5, and return May 13. That gives you ten calendar days away and eight hotel nights in Scotland. Move the dates below to compare early-May starts. The nonstop is seasonal; verify the exact flight dates and times before booking.

A few useful notes

Make the trip feel easy.

The best version of this itinerary is a little less ambitious than the map makes it look.

01

Hold the car until you leave Edinburgh

You can see the center on foot and by taxi. Pick up the car on the morning of the Highlands drive, then return it before the final city stay.

02

Plan for left-side driving

Rent an automatic if that’s more comfortable. Rural roads can be narrow, winding and slower than their mileage suggests. VisitScotland driving advice

03

Pack layers, not optimism

Early May can bring sun, showers and wind in the same afternoon. Waterproof outer layers and shoes you can walk in will earn their space.

04

Book the hard-to-move pieces first

Check the flight schedule and the two Skye/Highlands hotels first. Then settle the Edinburgh nights and dinner reservations around them.
